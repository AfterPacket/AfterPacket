## About Me

I build **open-source cybersecurity software** focused on helping defenders
understand attackers instead of simply blocking them.

My work centers on deception engineering, threat intelligence, AI security,
OSINT, anti-abuse systems, and large-scale internet measurement. Rather than
relying on third-party services, I build privacy-respecting, self-hosted tools
that give defenders complete visibility into their own infrastructure.

**Currently focused on:** high-interaction honeypots · ICS/SCADA deception ·
proof-of-work anti-abuse · LLM red-team automation · internet outage monitoring
